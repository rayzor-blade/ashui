# 3D assets

- `DamagedHelmet/`: Khronos glTF sample, CC BY-NC, by theblueturtle_ (see its README).
- `rogland_clear_night_2k.hdr`: Rogland Clear Night by Greg Zaal, Poly Haven, CC0.
- `studio_small_08_2k.hdr`: Studio Small 08 by Sergej Majboroda, Poly Haven, CC0.
- `sky_pano_-_milkyway/`: Sky Pano - Milkyway by MozillaHubs, Sketchfab, CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0 (see its license.txt); its panorama is a night sky.
- `marble_cliff_02_disp_1k.jpg`: Marble Cliff 02's displacement map by Amal Kumar, Poly Haven, CC0, a terrain's height map.
- `Mazda6/`: Free Mazda 6 Sedan 2011 AR/VR, LowPoly 3D Model by 3D_Mill, Sketchfab, CC-BY-4.0 (see its license.txt); the README banner's car. Modified: the maker's badges and script are painted out of its base colour texture, and the banner tints its paint and blanks the grille's badge in code.

The helmet and Rogland are copied from Blinc's `examples/blinc_app_examples/examples/assets/3d`;
the studio and displacement map from polyhaven.com. Blinc's Buster Drone (CC-BY-4.0)
and its `marble_cliff_02` glTF (CC0) are read from Blinc's checkout beside this one, not copied.
